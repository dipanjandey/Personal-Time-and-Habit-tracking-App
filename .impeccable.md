## Design Context

### Users
Knowledge workers and freelancers who track billable time, project work, and habits. They context-switch frequently and need at-a-glance clarity. Time is money — the interface should communicate that every moment is being tracked and accounted for.

### Brand Personality
Energetic · Bold · Urgent. Like a financial terminal for your workday — confident, immediate, no filler.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial dashboard. Barlow Condensed for display headings — tight, uppercase, massive. Geist for body copy. Deep forest-sage green (`oklch(0.42 0.195 162)`) as the dominant color, used sparingly for maximum impact. Light mode first. No glassmorphism, no gradient text, no rounded cards with drop shadows.

### Design Principles
1. **Urgency through scale** — numbers and headings should be dramatically larger than supporting text. 5x–7x size differences, not 1.5x.
2. **Green earns attention** — use the primary color only where it matters most: active states, TODAY's metrics, live indicators.
3. **Full-bleed over cards** — stat strips that extend edge-to-edge feel more editorial and less like a generic dashboard template.
4. **Sparse motion** — a pulse on a live indicator is enough; don't animate decoratively.
5. **Uppercase as anchor** — uppercase tracking labels (10px, letter-spacing 0.18–0.22em) create strong typographic hierarchy without requiring color or size.
